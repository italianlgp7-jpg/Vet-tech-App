Credential tiers on Tailshift
Credentialed Vet TechYOU

LVT · RVT · CVT · LVMT. License checked against the state board and re-verified before it expires. Sees every shift, including anesthesia, dental and other tech-scope work.

Veterinary Assistant

Experienced assistants. ID, work history and references verified; an approved assistant certificate is optional. Sees assistant-level shifts such as restraint, kennel, lab prep and client care.

Student / Uncredentialed Tech

Only where state law allows. Enrollment or experience verified. Shown only shifts in states that permit it, limited to tasks within that state's scope, and always labeled on the clinic's side.

    Credential tiers What each one can book
    Credentialed techYour tier

    LVT, RVT, CVT or LVMT. License checked with the state board and rechecked before it expires. Can book every shift, including anesthesia and dentals.

    Veterinary assistant

    ID, work history and two references checked. An assistant certificate is optional. Can book assistant-level shifts: restraint, kennel, lab prep, client care.

    Student or uncredentialed tech

    Enrollment or experience checked. Only sees shifts in states that allow it, limited to tasks within that state's rules. Clinics always see this label.
